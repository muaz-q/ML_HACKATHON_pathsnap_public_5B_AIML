PathSnap: Map-Matching & Route Reconstruction

UE24CS352A ML Hackathon · Team 5B AIML · Prakhruti, Meha, Shranaya, Mohammed Muaz Iqubal · github.com/muaz-q/ML_HACKATHON_pathsnap_public_5B_AIML

Problem & approach

We match noisy ~1 Hz GPS fixes to the road arc actually driven and output a connected route. For each fix we list its 12 nearest directed arcs as candidates. A classifier scores P(candidate = reference arc), and an HMM decoded with Viterbi picks the most likely connected arc sequence.

Data & preparation

80 train / 10 student-test trips (worldwide; each has its own road network): $tr_obs / $te_obs fixes. There are no missing values, but $tr_gap60 training trips have gaps > 60 s. Coordinates are projected per trip to metres (azimuthal equidistant). The untimed .route is turned into a per-fix label by a monotonic DP alignment. $tr_gt60 training fixes ($tr_gt60_pct) lie > 60 m from their arc (the track runs past the reference) and are excluded. 23 location-independent features per (fix, candidate) pair: distance and relative distance, heading agreement, temporal context (neighbouring fixes' distance to the same arc), geometry, motion and topology. Training table: $n_rows_train rows; candidate miss rate $miss_train.

Model comparison (GroupKFold-5 by trip)

$compact_table

Nearest-arc baseline: $nn_top1 Top-1. LightGBM was selected: best CV and test Top-1, best log loss (calibrated emissions for the HMM), fast inference.

Bayesian optimisation

Optuna TPE, $bo_trials trials, objective = mean grouped-CV Top-1 over 8 LightGBM hyperparameters (training trips only). Tuned vs untuned: CV Top-1 $bo_cv_u → $bo_cv_t; student-test Top-1 $bo_te_u → $bo_te_t; log loss $bo_ll_u → $bo_ll_t.

HMM / Viterbi

States: top-$hmm_k candidates per fix. Emission: renormalised classifier probability. Transition: exp(−|droute − dGPS|/β), with shortest paths from a bounded Dijkstra on the directed graph. Gaps > 60 s break the chain; unreachable pairs get a finite penalty. β = $hmm_beta, we = $hmm_w, tuned on out-of-fold training probabilities.

System

Laptop 1: FastAPI (/trips, /predict) loads the trip without its route, predicts, then scores server-side; ground truth never reaches the UI. Laptop 2: Leaflet UI showing the GPS, network, classifier-only and HMM routes, metrics and timings (~$avg_s s per trip).

Limitations

Labels come from an alignment of an untimed reference; arcs are straight segments; the true arc must be in the top-$hmm_k for the HMM to recover it.
