PathSnap: Map-Matching & Route Reconstruction

UE24CS352A ML Hackathon · Team 5B AIML · Prakhruti, Meha, Shranaya, Mohammed Muaz Iqubal · github.com/muaz-q/ML_HACKATHON_pathsnap_public_5B_AIML

Problem & approach

We match noisy ~1 Hz GPS fixes to the road arc actually driven and output a connected route. For each fix we list its 12 nearest directed arcs as candidates. A classifier scores P(candidate = reference arc), and an HMM decoded with Viterbi picks the most likely connected arc sequence.

Data & preparation

80 train / 10 student-test trips (worldwide; each has its own road network): 208,417 / 19,160 fixes. There are no missing values, but 22 training trips have gaps > 60 s. Coordinates are projected per trip to metres (azimuthal equidistant). The untimed .route is turned into a per-fix label by a monotonic DP alignment. 22,345 training fixes (10.7%) lie > 60 m from their arc (the track runs past the reference) and are excluded. 23 location-independent features per (fix, candidate) pair: distance and relative distance, heading agreement, temporal context (neighbouring fixes' distance to the same arc), geometry, motion and topology. Training table: 2,168,454 rows; candidate miss rate 0.89%.

Bayesian optimisation

Optuna TPE, 30 trials, objective = mean grouped-CV Top-1 over 8 LightGBM hyperparameters (training trips only). Tuned vs untuned: CV Top-1 89.96% → 90.30%; student-test Top-1 90.85% → 91.00%; log loss 0.0460 → 0.0438.

HMM / Viterbi

States: top-6 candidates per fix. Emission: renormalised classifier probability. Transition: exp(−|droute − dGPS|/β), with shortest paths from a bounded Dijkstra on the directed graph. Gaps > 60 s break the chain; unreachable pairs get a finite penalty. β = 3.0, we = 2.0, tuned on out-of-fold training probabilities.

System

Laptop 1: FastAPI (/trips, /predict) loads the trip without its route, predicts, then scores server-side; ground truth never reaches the UI. Laptop 2: Leaflet UI showing the GPS, network, classifier-only and HMM routes, metrics and timings (~2.5 s per trip).

Limitations

Labels come from an alignment of an untimed reference; arcs are straight segments; the true arc must be in the top-6 for the HMM to recover it.
